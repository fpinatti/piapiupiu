#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Functionally solid, but visually category-interchangeable. The current UI (`#090d16` background, `#111827` cards, `#1f2937` borders, `#10b981` emerald accents) reads like a generic dark-mode developer utility or crypto/QR scanner rather than a tabletop companion for **Wingspan**—a game celebrated for its naturalist field-guide typography, warm parchment/forest tones, and scientific ornithology. Furthermore, controls use raw Unicode characters (`▶`, `■`, `✕`) instead of drawn iconography, and top-anchored navigation forces awkward two-handed reach when a player is holding a bird card in one hand at the gaming table.

**Deterministic scan**: not applicable (native platform)

#### Overall Impression

The core loop—aiming at a card header or searching by PT-BR/EN/Latin name to hear real offline bird calls—is fast and genuinely useful at the table, but the interface is weighed down by stacked utility chrome, undersized touch targets, and a generic tech-utility aesthetic that misses the tactile world of *Wingspan*. The single biggest opportunity is decluttering the scanner viewport into a true one-handed, thumb-zone field-guide experience.

#### What's Working

1. **Bilingual + Scientific Taxonomic Hierarchy (`BirdPlayingCard.tsx` & `CatalogScreen.tsx`)**: Showing the primary language title alongside the secondary translation and italicized Latin binomial name (`Cyanocitta cristata`) directly solves the Brazilian mixed-edition table problem (Grok PT-BR + Stonemaier EN expansions).
2. **Scoped Aiming Reticle with Live Readout (`ScannerScreen.tsx`)**: The corner-bracketed target frame (`Posicione o topo da carta aqui`) paired with the live OCR text pill immediately teaches the user *why* they only need to frame the card's top header rather than the whole card.
3. **Graceful Hardware Degradation (`LiveCameraScanner.web.tsx` & `.native.tsx`)**: When permissions are denied or no camera is active, the interface transitions cleanly into an interactive test deck without dead-ending the user.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Undersized touch targets and top-anchored navigation break one-handed table use**
  - **Why it matters**: Players hold a *Wingspan* card in one hand and operate the phone with their thumb. In `App.tsx` (`styles.tabButton`, ~30dp high at the top right), `FilterBar.tsx` (`styles.langPill` / `styles.expPill`, `paddingVertical: 5`, ~28dp high), and `BirdPlayingCard.tsx` (`styles.closeBtn`, ~24dp), controls sit at the top of the screen and fall well below the 48×48dp (Android) / 44×44pt (iOS) minimum touch target size.
  - **Fix**: Move primary navigation (`Scanner` vs `Catálogo`) to a bottom navigation bar in the thumb zone, increase interactive pill/button hit targets to at least 44–48dp height (or use `hitSlop`), and replace raw Unicode glyphs (`▶`, `■`, `✕`) with consistent vector icons.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P1] Viewport crowding in `ScannerScreen` from 5+ stacked overlay bars**
  - **Why it matters**: On a phone screen, `ScannerScreen` simultaneously stacks the App Header, `FilterBar`, `quickActionsRow`, `cardTargetBox`, `simContainer`, `statusBanner` (on Web), and `BirdPlayingCard`. This leaves almost no breathing room for the camera feed and fails the Cognitive Load checklist on **Single focus** and **Visual hierarchy** (3 checklist failures = moderate-to-high extraneous load).
  - **Fix**: Collapse secondary filters (`FilterBar`) and the OCR Simulator (`simContainer`) behind a compact bottom sheet or expandable drawer when the camera is active, keeping only the camera reticle, a single status/torch pill, and the detected `BirdPlayingCard` visible during active scanning.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P2] Generic dark-tech visual palette instead of an ornithological / *Wingspan* identity**
  - **Why it matters**: Default Tailwind slate/gray (`#111827`, `#1f2937`, `#9ca3af`) and neon green (`#4ade80`) feel like a barcode debugger. Secondary text `#6b7280` on `#111827` (`ocrSourceText` in `BirdPlayingCard.tsx` and `countText` in `CatalogScreen.tsx`) also drops to ~3.7:1 contrast, below the 4.5:1 legibility floor.
  - **Fix**: Shift the dark table-friendly palette toward deep forest/night-sky tones with warm parchment/ivory foregrounds, habitat-tinted accents (forest moss, grassland ochre, wetland teal), and lift muted text tokens above 4.5:1 contrast.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P2] Silent 6-second debounce and missing "Recently Scanned" history at the table**
  - **Why it matters**: When a player scans a card, passes the phone to a friend 4 seconds later, and the friend scans the same card, `shouldTriggerBirdAudio` silently blocks playback. Without a visual cooldown ring/bar or a "Recent cards" strip, the second player thinks the scanner is broken.
  - **Fix**: Show a subtle 6-second cooldown indicator or allow tapping the already-visible `BirdPlayingCard` / a compact "Últimas aves lidas" history chip row to replay immediately.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable delight`

**Jordan (First-Timer at a *Wingspan* Game Night)**:

- Taps the last active expansion chip in `FilterBar.tsx` (`handleToggleExpansion`) and nothing happens—no hint explains that at least one expansion must remain active.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent / Low-Vision User)**:
- `ocrSourceText` (`#6b7280` on `#111827`, 11px) in `BirdPlayingCard.tsx` and `countText` (`#6b7280` on `#090d16`, 12px) in `CatalogScreen.tsx` fail WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast.
- Play/Stop buttons in `CatalogScreen.tsx` (`▶` / `■`) and close button (`✕`) in `BirdPlayingCard.tsx` lack `accessibilityLabel` and `accessibilityRole="button"` props for TalkBack/VoiceOver.

#### Minor Observations

- **Missing habitats in `CatalogScreen.tsx`**: `birds.json` includes `habitats: ["forest", "grassland", "wetland"]`, and `BirdPlayingCard` renders them (`Floresta`, `Campo`, `Água`), but `CatalogScreen` rows omit them completely.
- **Hardcoded font sizes without typographic scale**: Sizes jump between `10`, `11`, `12`, `13`, `14`, `15`, `16`, `18`, `19` across components instead of following a cohesive type scale.
- **No persistent mini-player in `CatalogScreen`**: If you tap a bird at the top of the catalog and scroll down, you can't see which bird is playing or stop it without scrolling back up.
